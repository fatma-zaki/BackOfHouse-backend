01

Product overview

نظرة عامة على المنتج

Back Of House is a national professional platform for the Saudi events sector. It connects individuals and companies through profiles, company portfolios, opportunities, supplier requests, events, news and rewards. The PRD ships the verified companies directory first. The app has since been built in a different order: the individual experience and the company workspace run end to end on mock data, while the Phase 1 directory, search and claim flows are still missing.

What changed since the 14 Sep map

  • Two account types are settled. INDIVIDUAL and COMPANY. Students are individuals with profileStatus = STUDENT. Supplier, venue and organizer are a companyType.
  • Navigation is settled. Individuals get Home · Discover · News · Rewards · Profile. Companies get Dashboard · Opportunities · Supplier Requests · Portfolio · Profile under /company. The Community tab is gone.
  • No member posts. News is editorial. Members read, save and share.
  • Opportunities and applications are specified and built. One Opportunity model with 5 types, one application pipeline, and a separate B2B SupplierResponse.
  • The company workspace is built. It covers listings reviewed by Back Of House, applicants, supplier requests and responses, the portfolio and the company profile.
  • Rewards is built. The catalog, redemption and points history all work, and Profile Highlight is approved as a points reward.
  • The build is healthy. flutter analyze reports no issues and all 109 tests pass (1 skipped). The broken design-system screen is fixed.

Reality check before design continues

  • The PRD still defers a native app, and this repo is one. §2.2 and §26 put a native app after launch, and §19 recommends an SSR web front end. See NAV-1.
  • Almost everything runs on mock data. Only auth calls the API. The other 10 data layers carry a TODO(api). ApiEndpoints declares no endpoints for opportunities, applications, companies, supplier requests, rewards or notifications, so the backend contract is still to be written.
  • Every listing needs a Back Of House review, and no review tool exists. The app shows a development-only "Simulate approval" button in its place.
  • The approved rules say "bottom navigation", but both shells use a side drawer (NavDrawerShell). See NAV-7.
  • Phase 1 directory items are missing. Company directory, public company profile, search, filters and claim. Home and Discover already link to the directory placeholders.

What the approved shape implies

  • Events, opportunities, companies, professionals and courses all open from Discover.
  • News gets a tab of its own because there is no member content.
  • Rewards gets a tab: points are for rewards, and Profile Highlight lives there.
  • Notifications open from Home and from the bell. My applications opens from Profile, from an opportunity and after applying.

Account-aware routing

  • A company that opens an individual link lands on its dashboard, and the reverse holds too.
  • There is no workspace switcher, so one account is one experience.
  • Signed-out deep links go to /auth?from=… and come back after sign-in.
04

Individual user flow

رحلة الفرد

Sign-up now follows the business rules: one form, then Individual or Company, and individuals can tick "I'm currently a student". The PRD §5.1 steps are still missing: one-time code, consent preferences, basic profile completion and a verification request. The dashed edge shows where they would go.

Account types now follow the rules

The code uses AccountType (individual · company) and ProfileStatus (professional · student). Old member_type values are still read. The PRD's five types collapse into these two, except the entity representative. See INS-1.

Verification is still thin

Individuals carry verified and a numeric verificationLevel. The PRD defines states: not started · under review · needs info · accepted · rejected · expired. Nobody can request verification in the app yet.

Contact privacy has no home

Companies see an applicant's contact details only from CONTACT, and only the fields the applicant shares. Sharing defaults to nothing, and no settings screen exists yet. Until one does, no company will ever see contact details.

05

Company user flow

رحلة الشركة

A company is created at sign-up with a name and a companyType, and lands on its dashboard. Verification happens outside the app and appears read-only. It is not a prerequisite for anything in the MVP. The PRD's claim flow for imported profiles isn't built. See ID-5.

Owner, permissions and capabilities

The MVP has one Owner per company. Screens and data sources ask for a CompanyPermission, never a role, so adding the PRD's Editor and Analyst later won't touch them. A CompanyCapability is separate from permissions. It unlocks supplier requests for organizers, agencies, venues and production companies, or when the API grants it.

06

Events flow

رحلة الفعاليات

Events live in Discover. The list, the details and registration all work on mock data, and registering correctly earns no points. Everything after registration is missing. The pass, calendar, maps, save and share buttons show "coming soon", and nothing in the app verifies attendance. That is where the PRD awards points.

Registration التسجيل

This is a promise to attend. In the mock it toggles isRegistered, updates the attendee count and issues a pass code. It never touches the points balance.

Labels to check

Accredited events show "GEA Verified", which reuses the word the rules reserve for Back Of House trust. Event details also show a "fit" score from the data. See COM-6, EVT-4.

Not specified

Who checks people in, and with which tool (EVT-2). Paid tickets are out of the MVP.

07

Opportunities flow

رحلة الفرص والطلبات

One Opportunity model covers five types. JOB, PROJECT, CO_OP and VOLUNTEERING are for individuals. SUPPLIER_REQUEST is B2B only. The PRD's temporary-contract and freelance types become a job's EmploymentType. Every company listing goes through Back Of House review, whether or not the company is verified. The company decides on applicants, and Back Of House only connects the two sides.

Listing lifecycle OpportunityLifecycle

Individual application ApplicationRules

Who may apply · eligibilityFor

  • Individuals only. Companies never apply as individuals.
  • Only open, published listings.
  • CO_OP is for STUDENT profiles only.
  • Verification isn't required.

Applying again

An active, accepted or rejected application blocks a new one to the same listing. Only a withdrawn application allows a new one. The data layer enforces this, and the screen explains it.

What is not in an application

No CV upload, no interview scheduling, no offers and no messaging. Match scores come only from the backend, and the app never computes one.

12

Product gaps

الفجوات في المنتج

These are places where the requirements are too thin to design against, the code contradicts the PRD or the rules, or a built screen leads nowhere. Each links to a decision in section 13.

Backend & data 🔴

  • Only auth calls the API. Ten data layers are mock-only (TODO(api)).
  • ApiEndpoints has no contract for opportunities, applications, applicants, company, supplier requests, rewards or notifications.
  • The token refresh endpoint is declared but unused. A 401 clears the session.
  • No push delivery for application status notifications.

Back-office 🔴

  • Every listing and revision needs a Back Of House review, and there is no tool. The app has a dev-only "Simulate approval".
  • Company verification is "handled outside the app" without a defined owner or tool.
  • Rewards catalog, point rules and company capabilities have no admin.

Platform & navigation 🟣

  • The PRD defers a native app, and this repo is Flutter mobile.
  • The rules say bottom navigation, and the code uses a drawer.
  • The PRD lets visitors search publicly. The app requires sign-in for every screen.
  • One person who is both a professional and a company representative needs two accounts.

Identity & profile 🔴

  • One-time code (AUTH-01), consent preferences, MFA and session management are missing.
  • Edit profile says "coming soon". Nobody can update experience, projects, skills or student status after sign-up.
  • There's no contact-privacy setting, so contact details are never released.
  • Google sign-in shows "coming soon" and isn't in the PRD.

Directory & search 🔴

  • The companies list, public company profile, professionals list and professional profile are placeholders. Home, Discover and opportunity cards lead into them.
  • Discover search only searches events, and its filter button opens Events.
  • The claim flow and the duplicate check for imported companies are unaddressed.
  • Company projects can't hold images yet.

Built without a rule 🟣

  • Follow company earns +5 points in the mock ledger. The PRD says low-quality interaction earns nothing.
  • Connect with professionals implies a social graph that nothing else uses.
  • Event details show a "fit" score. The rules cover match scores only for opportunities.
  • "GEA Verified" on events reuses the word reserved for trust.

Points & rewards 🔴

  • The level is computed from the spendable balance, so redeeming can drop a level. The model's comment says lifetime points.
  • The code has 4 levels (100 · 500 · 1500) and the PRD has 6, including Verified professional.
  • There are no caps, rule versions, reversals, appeals or badge catalog. Profile "badges" are certifications.
  • Redemption codes are generated by the mock, and there's no list of past redemptions.

Events 🟡

  • View pass, add to calendar, open maps, save and share all show "coming soon".
  • No check-in, so attendance points can't be earned for real.
  • No "My events" list, waitlist or transfer.
